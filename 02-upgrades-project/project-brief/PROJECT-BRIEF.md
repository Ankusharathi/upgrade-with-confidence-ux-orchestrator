# Upgrade With Confidence

## Evidence status

This is a synthetic UX project created for an academic demonstration. Any research, quotations, analytics, usability findings, business metrics, and outcomes produced for this project must be clearly labeled as synthetic. They must not be presented as findings from real Verizon customers or systems.

## Problem statement

In a synthetic telecom upgrade journey, eligible customers begin upgrading their device but do not finish because they cannot clearly understand what they will pay today, what their new monthly bill will be, how the trade-in affects the price, and whether their plan will change.

Design a clear, guided upgrade summary that gives customers confidence in the total cost and requirements before they commit, helping more customers complete the upgrade digitally.

## Design challenge

How might we make the complete cost and impact of an upgrade clear enough that eligible customers can confidently finish their purchase without contacting support?

## Primary conversion goal

Increase the percentage of eligible customers who move from starting an upgrade to a confirmed order.

## Supporting outcomes

- Reduce abandonment at review and checkout.
- Increase adoption of eligible trade-in offers.
- Reduce uncertainty about monthly and upfront costs.
- Reduce upgrade-related support contacts.
- Increase successful completion of the self-service journey.

## Measurement note

Any improvement figures used in this project will be framed as synthetic targets or simulated results, not real business performance.
