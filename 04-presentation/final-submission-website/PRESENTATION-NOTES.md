# V2 Presentation Notes

## Opening

“AI can make UX work faster. It can also make the reasoning disappear. This project demonstrates a six-phase Orchestrator that keeps the evidence, AI contribution, artifact and human decision connected.”

## Audit transition

“The redesign starts with one unanswered question: what will I actually pay? We did not replace the supplied upgrade journey. We evaluated it with Nielsen’s heuristics, preserved its familiar patterns and added the missing financial layer.”

## Prototype

“The proposed flow retains line selection, device browsing, PDP configuration, color, payment, plan, protection, trade-in, review and confirmation. Upgrade Impact now shows both the projected bill and the change from the current bill, with the calculation visible.”

## Testing boundary

“The workflow has been evaluated, but no customer preference study has been completed. The next study compares comprehension and reasoned preference across the current and proposed experiences.”

## Product-owner close

“Clarity is not the business result; it is the mechanism we can test. The primary signal is review-to-submit conversion. Support exits, billing contacts, cancellations, returns and promotion disputes are guardrails, because a completed order is not successful if it produces surprise afterward.”
