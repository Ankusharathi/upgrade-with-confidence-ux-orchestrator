# Evidence Boundary

This folder prevents synthetic demonstration material from being mistaken for real customer or business evidence.

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [`synthetic-data/`](synthetic-data/) | Identifies invented examples, simulated inputs, and workflow-validation records |
| [`assumptions/`](assumptions/) | Separates design and business hypotheses from supported facts |
| [`limitations/`](limitations/) | States what the project cannot claim and what evidence is required next |

## Evidence classes used in this submission

- **Publicly supportable:** upgrade transactions can involve different charges, credits, conditions, and timelines.
- **Expert evaluation:** heuristic findings identify potential usability risks, not observed customer behavior.
- **Synthetic demonstration:** prices, customer details, analytics, quotes, sessions, and validation records are invented or simulated for academic use.
- **Hypothesis:** improved financial comprehension may reduce hesitation and support completion, conversion, and retained upgrades.
- **Not established:** real customer preference, conversion lift, retention lift, accessibility conformance, or production feasibility.
