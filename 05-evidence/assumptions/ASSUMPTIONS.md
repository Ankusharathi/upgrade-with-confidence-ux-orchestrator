# Assumptions and Hypotheses

1. Customers may find it difficult to assemble upgrade costs that occur today, on the next bill, and after conditional credits begin.
2. A persistent Upgrade Impact summary may improve pre-commitment financial comprehension.
3. Better comprehension may reduce hesitation, backtracking, support exits, billing surprises, and cancellations.
4. Preserving familiar shopping patterns while adding a financial layer may be more adaptable than replacing the entire journey.
5. A grounded public-information guide may answer general questions without entering the transaction or accessing account data.

These statements guide design and research. They are not findings. The comparative study and controlled product measurement plan describe how they would be tested.
