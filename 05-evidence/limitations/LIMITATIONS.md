# Limitations

- No real customers participated in the demonstrated validation.
- No production analytics, conversion baseline, retention data, support-contact data, or cancellation data were available.
- Heuristic evaluation indicates potential risk; it does not measure prevalence or customer preference.
- Workflow and accessibility checks do not establish WCAG conformance.
- The prototype does not prove production feasibility, billing accuracy, promotion eligibility, or integration behavior.
- Upgrade Guide has no access to accounts, orders, bills, live inventory, or private offers.
- Conversion and retention are proposed measurement outcomes, not achieved results.

## Evidence required next

1. Comparative comprehension testing with representative participants.
2. Accessibility evaluation with assistive technologies and disabled participants where appropriate.
3. Technical and operational feasibility review.
4. Controlled review-to-submit experiment with cancellation, return, support, and billing guardrails.
