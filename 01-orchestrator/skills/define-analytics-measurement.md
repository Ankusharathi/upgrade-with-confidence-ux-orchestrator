# Skill: Define Analytics and Measurement

## Purpose

Specify privacy-aware analytics needed to verify implementation and monitor approved outcomes after release.

## Method

Map each outcome or operational question to events, properties, triggers, exclusions, identity and consent rules, data classification, owner, destination, validation method, baseline or target status, and retention. Define duplicate, retry, failure, recovery, and completion semantics. Separate proposed instrumentation from implemented and verified instrumentation.

## Guardrails

Do not invent event availability, KPI baselines, targets, consent, data access, or production verification. Analytics acceptance remains owned by named product, data, privacy, and engineering roles.
