# Conduct Responsible Research

## Entry conditions

Proceed only when the Research Plan identifies an accountable owner and execution authorization explicitly covers the proposed recruitment, contact, recording, data, incentive, and storage activities.

## Execution

- Follow the approved protocol while allowing neutral follow-up questions.
- Obtain informed consent and recording permission where applicable.
- Support accessibility and voluntary participation without coercion.
- Collect only data needed for the research questions.
- Use anonymized identifiers in notes and working artifacts.
- Record session or collection status, deviations, incidents, withdrawals, and unusable data without concealment.
- Update the Evidence Register with each usable source or record.

## Stop conditions

Stop and route to the accountable owner if authorization is absent, consent is withdrawn, handling requirements cannot be met, participant safety is at risk, the protocol changes materially, or collected data falls outside approved scope.

## Rules

- Never fabricate or reconstruct missing observations or quotations.
- Do not contact, recruit, schedule, or compensate people without the corresponding authorization.
- Do not store credentials, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information in research artifacts.

## Output

Update `../templates/EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md`, record plan deviations, and return the collected evidence for synthesis. Execution alone does not create approved findings.
