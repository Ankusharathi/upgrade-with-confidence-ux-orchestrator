# Manage Research Evidence

## Goal

Maintain a traceable evidence base that shows what was reviewed, what each source can support, and where uncertainty remains.

## Method

For each source or raw record, capture:

- a stable identifier, source, owner, type, and date;
- access and verification status;
- linked research questions;
- what the evidence supports and does not support;
- population, context, recency, quality, and limitations when relevant;
- sensitivity classification, storage location, retention, and access restrictions;
- related finding identifiers after synthesis.

Assess gaps by question, not merely by missing document type. Deduplicate references without erasing distinct contexts or contradictory evidence.

## Rules

- Stakeholder statements remain business inputs unless supported by user evidence.
- Do not copy unnecessary personal or sensitive information into the register.
- Use anonymized participant identifiers and approved storage locations.
- A missing or inaccessible source stays visible; do not infer its contents.

## Output

Create and maintain the Evidence Register using `../templates/EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md`.
