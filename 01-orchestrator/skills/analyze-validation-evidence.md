# Skill: Analyze Validation Evidence

## Purpose

Turn authorized session, expert, accessibility, technical, and product evidence into criterion-level results without overstating what the evidence supports.

## Method

1. Verify source eligibility, completeness, protocol version, tested artifact, and deviations.
2. Normalize task and criterion records while preserving source identifiers and successful cases.
3. Separate direct observation, participant words, measured values, interpretation, and recommendation.
4. Calculate task or error measures only when definitions, denominators, and data quality permit; label small-sample results descriptively.
5. Compare patterns across roles, scenarios, devices, accessibility needs, and states without demographic stereotyping.
6. Preserve contradictions and outliers that expose consequence or unmet need.
7. Rate confidence from method fit, source quality, consistency, sample coverage, fidelity, and deviation impact.
8. Mark each criterion `met`, `partially-met`, `not-met`, or `untested` with linked evidence.

## Output

An evidence inventory and finding set in which every claim is traceable, limitations are explicit, and missing evidence remains missing.
