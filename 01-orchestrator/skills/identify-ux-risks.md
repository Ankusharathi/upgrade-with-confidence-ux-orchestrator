# Identify UX Risks

## Goal

Make material uncertainty and harm visible early enough to shape Discover and human review.

## Risk lenses

Check only relevant lenses:

- user value and problem-framing risk;
- accessibility and exclusion risk;
- privacy, consent, sensitive-data, and participant-safety risk;
- business, legal, policy, or compliance dependency;
- technical or operational feasibility dependency;
- evidence quality and sampling risk;
- timeline, ownership, and decision-authority risk;
- scope, cross-channel, localization, and service dependency.

## Method

For each material risk, record:

- stable risk identifier;
- risk statement in `If ... then ... because ...` form;
- basis: evidence, supplied claim, assumption, or unknown;
- likelihood and impact as `low`, `medium`, `high`, or `unknown`;
- confidence as `low`, `medium`, or `high`;
- owner, or `unassigned`;
- next treatment: clarify in Intake, investigate in Discover, obtain specialist review, accept, or block;
- linked evidence, question, or decision identifiers.

## Escalation

Recommend `blocked` when proceeding would be unsafe, unauthorized, or impossible without a required input. Recommend `human-decision` for consequential risk acceptance, ownership, scope, or policy choices. Otherwise carry the risk into Discover.

## Rules

- A risk is not a finding.
- Unknown likelihood is preferable to invented precision.
- Do not provide legal, medical, security, or accessibility certification; identify the need for an accountable specialist when relevant.
