# Frame Research Study

## Goal

Define the smallest research frame that can reduce uncertainty for the approved decision without silently changing project scope.

## Method

1. Record the approved brief, decision, owners, users, scope, constraints, risks, and carried-forward questions.
2. Separate known context, stakeholder input, assumptions, hypotheses, and missing evidence.
3. Rewrite broad questions into answerable research questions tied to a decision consequence.
4. For each question, state the evidence needed and what would remain unresolved if it is not obtained.
5. Prioritize questions by decision impact, risk, and timing; do not prioritize by stakeholder curiosity alone.

## Rules

- Do not turn stakeholder claims or desired features into user findings.
- Keep scope changes as proposed decisions requiring human approval.
- Mark questions that require policy, technical, legal, or business answers rather than user research.
- Use the approved terms and stable identifiers from Intake.

## Output

Populate the decision, objective, research questions, known context, assumptions, evidence needs, and scope sections of `../templates/RESEARCH-PLAN.md`.
