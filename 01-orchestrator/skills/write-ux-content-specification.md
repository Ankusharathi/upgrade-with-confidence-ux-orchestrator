# Write UX Content Specification

## Goal

Make product language traceable, consistent, actionable, accessible, and ready for review across normal and recovery states.

## Method

1. Define voice, terminology, reading and comprehension needs, and content principles from approved context.
2. Inventory headings, labels, instructions, controls, status, errors, recovery, confirmation, help, disclosures, and notifications by state ID.
3. Record exact copy when approved; otherwise use a clearly labeled draft or content requirement.
4. Define variants by role, permission, outcome, channel, locale, and accessibility need.
5. Record source, owner, legal or policy dependency, localization need, character constraint, and validation status.

## Rules

- Do not invent policy, price, eligibility, legal, privacy, security, or promotional claims.
- Errors identify what happened and a safe next action when disclosure is permitted.
- Button labels describe the action and consequence.
- Placeholder text does not replace a persistent label.

## Output

Create `../templates/UX-CONTENT-SPECIFICATION.md` and link content IDs to states and validation tasks.
