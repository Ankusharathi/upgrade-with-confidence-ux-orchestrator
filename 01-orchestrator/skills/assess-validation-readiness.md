# Skill: Assess Validation Readiness

## Purpose

Determine whether the planned validation can safely and credibly answer its questions before evidence collection begins.

## Checks

- approved design and exact prototype or build version;
- fidelity for every criterion and explicit simulated behavior;
- task paths, branches, errors, recovery, content, and test data;
- device, browser, network, account, role, permission, and environment setup;
- logging and instrumentation behavior, including known gaps;
- keyboard, zoom, screen reader, alternative input, captioning, and other required accessibility setup;
- recruiting criteria, scheduling, consent, recording, incentive, privacy, storage, and deletion authorization;
- moderator script, observers, note capture, incident path, dry run, backup, abort, and reschedule rules;
- named owners for unresolved product, engineering, content, accessibility, legal, and operational questions.

## Result

Classify each check as `ready`, `conditional`, `blocked`, or `not-applicable`. The overall result is `ready` only when no blocked item can invalidate evidence or harm participants. A conditional item includes its owner, constraint, and effect on interpretation.
