# Specify Accessible Design

## Goal

Define accessibility behavior early enough to shape the experience and be verified during validation and implementation.

## Method

1. Confirm applicable organizational standards, platforms, jurisdictions, user groups, and specialist owners.
2. Map requirements to state, component, content, and task-flow IDs.
3. Specify semantic structure, names and roles, labels and instructions, keyboard behavior, focus order and movement, status announcements, error identification and suggestion, review and correction, contrast, zoom, reflow, target size, motion, media, time limits, and authentication as applicable.
4. Define accessible alternatives, accommodations, test method, expected result, and responsible owner.
5. Record unresolved applicability and specialist-review needs.

## Rules

- A specification is not conformance evidence or certification.
- Do not use “accessible” as an untestable acceptance criterion.
- Do not defer all accessibility work to implementation.
- Preserve conflicts with security, privacy, legal, or platform requirements for specialist decision.

## Output

Create `../templates/ACCESSIBILITY-DESIGN-SPECIFICATION.md` with testable requirements and verification ownership.
