# Industry UX Standards Review

## Goal

Translate applicable external standards and mature conventions into traceable UX requirements and review questions without overstating legal or compliance conclusions.

## Method

1. Define product, industry, jurisdiction, market, platform, user groups, data types, and journey stages.
2. Identify applicable normative standards, regulatory or policy requirements, platform guidance, and evidence-based conventions.
3. Prefer current official sources and record title, publisher, version, publication or update date, accessed date, jurisdiction, and status.
4. Map each applicable provision or principle to the affected UX surface, requirement, rationale, evidence, and accountable specialist.
5. Classify each item as required, organizational policy, recommended guidance, common convention, or unresolved applicability.
6. Record conflicts, outdated guidance, implementation ambiguity, and specialist-review needs.

## Rules

- Browse current authoritative sources when executing this skill because standards and regulations change.
- Distinguish mandatory requirements from recommendations and common patterns.
- Do not claim legal, privacy, security, or accessibility certification.
- Do not use a competitor pattern as an industry standard without independent support.
- Route uncertain applicability to the accountable legal, compliance, privacy, security, or accessibility owner.

## Output

Create `../templates/UX-STANDARDS-REVIEW.md` and register every cited source in the Evidence Register.
