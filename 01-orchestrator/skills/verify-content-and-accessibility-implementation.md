# Skill: Verify Content and Accessibility Implementation

## Purpose

Check implemented content and specified accessibility behavior using appropriate evidence and specialist boundaries.

## Method

Verify approved strings, variants, truncation, localization readiness, dynamic values, error and recovery content, ownership, and legal or commercial status. Check applicable semantics, names and roles, keyboard, focus, status announcements, errors, contrast, zoom, reflow, motion, target size, timing, and assistive-technology behavior. Link every result to the requirement, build, environment, evidence, and defect.

## Guardrails

Do not infer accessibility conformance from visual fidelity, automated tools, or incomplete environments. Preserve content and accessibility specialist review status.
