# Skill: Frame Validation Plan

## Purpose

Turn the approved Design validation handoff into the smallest credible plan that can support the pending decision.

## Inputs

- approved Design Specification, gate decision, handoff, hypotheses, criteria, risks, and limitations;
- available prototype or build, evidence sources, participant access, specialists, environments, timeline, and authorization.

## Method

1. State the decision Validate must support and what would change because of the result.
2. Inventory each approved validation question and criterion; do not create substitute criteria silently.
3. Map each criterion to the lightest credible method, evidence source, population or specialist, environment, and analysis rule.
4. Define inclusion, exclusion, sample rationale, role and accessibility coverage, and limits on inference.
5. Define success, partial success, failure, critical error, abort, escalation, and untested states before execution.
6. Record authorizations, privacy, consent, recording, incentives, sensitive data, storage, deletion, and incident handling.
7. Declare dependencies, risks, deviations requiring reapproval, and stop conditions.

## Output requirements

- every criterion has a method and source or is explicitly untestable;
- sample size is a rationale, not a promise of representativeness;
- participant research is `planned`, not `executed`, until evidence exists;
- unresolved authorization produces `blocked`, not fabricated data;
- plan approval and phase approval are separate decisions.
