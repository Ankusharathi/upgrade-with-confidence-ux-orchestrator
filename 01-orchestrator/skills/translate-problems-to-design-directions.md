# Translate Problems to Design Directions

## Goal

Frame coherent experience directions that respond to approved problems while keeping alternatives, assumptions, trade-offs, and evidence boundaries visible.

## Method

1. Verify the approved problem, opportunity, outcome, scope, constraint, and decision identifiers.
2. State design principles and non-negotiable requirements derived from approved inputs.
3. Generate materially different directions when alternatives could change user control, information structure, channel, sequence, or operational dependency.
4. For each direction, map addressed problems and outcomes, design hypotheses, trade-offs, risks, dependencies, accessibility implications, and validation needs.
5. Record selection, deferral, or rejection rationale and the accountable decision owner.

## Rules

- A selected direction is approved intent, not validated effectiveness.
- Do not convert a feature request into strategy without problem and outcome traceability.
- Do not claim expected lift, user preference, feasibility, or accessibility conformance without evidence.
- Return scope or problem changes to Define.

## Output

Create `../templates/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md` and carry the selected direction and alternatives into the Design Specification.
