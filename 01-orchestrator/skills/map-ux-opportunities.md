# Map UX Opportunities

## Goal

Show the full opportunity landscape before prioritization so the team can see relationships, conflicts, gaps, and sequencing without collapsing strategy into a feature list.

## Method

1. Start only from supported problem statements and eligible insights.
2. Define each opportunity as a change in user, service, operational, or business outcome.
3. Link affected roles, journey stages, problem IDs, insight IDs, finding IDs, evidence IDs, risks, and desired outcomes.
4. Record relationships such as prerequisite, enabling, overlapping, competing, mutually exclusive, or independent.
5. Identify scope boundaries, constraints, dependencies, accessibility and inclusion implications, guardrails, and unresolved specialist decisions.
6. Separate evidence-supported opportunities from hypotheses and unsupported feature requests.

## Rules

- Do not name a component, channel, workflow pattern, or technology unless it is a constraint rather than a proposed solution.
- Do not treat opportunity breadth as priority.
- Preserve tensions between user and business outcomes.
- Keep weak-evidence areas visible as `investigate` rather than silently discarding them.

## Output

Create `../templates/OPPORTUNITY-MAP.md` and pass eligible opportunities to prioritization.
