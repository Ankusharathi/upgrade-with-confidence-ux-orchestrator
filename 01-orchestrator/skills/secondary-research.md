# Secondary Research

## Goal

Use credible existing evidence to answer research questions, refine hypotheses, and identify gaps before proposing new primary research.

## Method

1. Define the questions, population, context, geography, date range, and inclusion and exclusion criteria.
2. Search internal sources and current external sources using documented queries or source routes.
3. Prefer original research, official standards, authoritative data, and primary sources over summaries.
4. Record author or organization, title, date, URL or repository location, accessed date, evidence type, population, method, and relevance.
5. Assess credibility, recency, applicability, conflicts of interest, methodological limits, and transferability.
6. Synthesize converging, conflicting, and missing evidence without converting correlation into causation.

## Rules

- Browse for current external evidence when this skill is executed; cite direct sources rather than search-result pages.
- Distinguish empirical evidence, expert guidance, market commentary, stakeholder input, and hypothesis.
- Do not use an abstract or summary to claim details not verified in the source.
- Do not generalize findings to a different population or context without stating the limitation.
- Respect access, copyright, confidentiality, and quotation limits.

## Output

Create `../templates/SECONDARY-RESEARCH.md` and register every included source in the Evidence Register.
