# Select Research Methods

## Goal

Choose methods based on the decision and evidence needed rather than habit or a fixed sample-size rule.

## Method selection

- Use existing-evidence review before proposing new collection.
- Use interviews or contextual inquiry for behaviors, needs, context, and mental models.
- Use usability testing for defined tasks and evaluative questions.
- Use surveys only when sampling and question design support the intended quantitative inference.
- Use analytics for observable behavioral patterns while documenting instrumentation and population limits.
- Combine methods only when one method cannot credibly answer the priority questions.

For every method, record linked questions, rationale, sources or participants, inclusion and exclusion criteria, sample rationale, procedure, analysis approach, limitations, dependencies, and stopping condition.

## Rules

- Do not claim representativeness from convenience or small qualitative samples.
- Include relevant accessibility and edge-case needs without stereotyping participants.
- Do not recruit or schedule people while selecting a method.
- Escalate feasibility, incentive, sensitive-data, or specialist-review decisions to the accountable owner.

## Output

Complete the methods, sources or participants, procedure, analysis, timeline, risks, and limitations in `../templates/RESEARCH-PLAN.md`.
