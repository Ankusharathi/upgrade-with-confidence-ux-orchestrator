# Model Future-State Experience

## Goal

Create a coherent proposed experience structure before screen-level specification.

## Method

1. Set Journey Map and Task Flow mode to `future-state` and IA mode to `proposed`.
2. Link every proposed stage, object, label, navigation path, node, branch, recovery, and handoff to an approved problem, outcome, requirement, decision, or clearly labeled hypothesis.
3. Cover roles, permissions, account or segment variation, channels, devices, operational handoffs, failure, interruption, recovery, confirmation, and post-task activity.
4. Cross-check terms, objects, node IDs, states, and success conditions across all three artifacts.
5. Record technical, policy, content, accessibility, analytics, and operational dependencies plus validation criteria.

## Rules

- Future-state models are proposed designs, not observed journeys.
- Do not reuse current-state pain points or emotions as future-state evidence.
- Do not invent backend rules or approved labels.
- Keep service and system dependencies visible.

## Output

Produce linked `JOURNEY-MAP.md`, `INFORMATION-ARCHITECTURE.md`, and `TASK-FLOW.md` artifacts through the UX Journey Architecture Agent.
