# Prioritize UX Opportunities

## Goal

Recommend which supported problem or outcome spaces deserve Design attention first while keeping evidence strength and uncertainty visible.

## Method

1. Express each opportunity as an outcome space linked to one or more approved problem statements.
2. Evaluate decision relevance, evidence strength, user consequence, business alignment, risk or urgency, accessibility and inclusion impact, dependency readiness, and feasibility uncertainty.
3. Use explicit ordinal ratings such as `high`, `medium`, `low`, or `unknown`; define them in the artifact.
4. Record rationale, supporting and contradicting evidence, dependencies, assumptions, and specialist decisions.
5. Assign a priority band and explain why; preserve ties and disagreements when evidence does not support a strict order.
6. Identify opportunities that must return to Discover or await a human decision.

## Rules

- Prioritize opportunities, not unapproved features or stakeholder favorites.
- Do not multiply arbitrary scores into false precision.
- Low feasibility confidence is not proof of low value; keep value and feasibility distinct.
- An opportunity with weak evidence cannot become high confidence through business urgency alone.
- Preserve accessibility, safety, privacy, and irreversible-harm considerations even when frequency is unknown.

## Output

Create `../templates/OPPORTUNITY-PRIORITIZATION.md` and link each priority to problem, insight, finding, evidence, risk, and decision identifiers.
