# Skill: Triage UX QA Defects

## Purpose

Classify, route, and track UX defects from discovery through verified resolution or authorized disposition.

## Method

Record expected and observed behavior, reproduction steps, environment, evidence, affected users and states, consequence, recoverability, frequency in tested coverage, severity, confidence, source requirement, owner, route, target fix, dependency, and retest. Classify implementation defect, design defect, content defect, accessibility defect, analytics defect, environment defect, expected approved variance, duplicate, or unresolved.

## Guardrails

The agent cannot accept risk, defer a blocker, or close a defect without fix verification or a traceable authorized decision. Severity and priority remain distinct.
