# Skill: Control Delivery Changes and Revalidation

## Purpose

Assess implementation-driven changes and determine required approval, artifact updates, QA, and revalidation.

## Method

Record the proposed or observed change, initiator, reason, affected requirements, flows, states, content, accessibility, data, analytics, validation criteria, risk, and source versions. Classify as clarification, equivalent implementation, minor variance, material design change, scope change, or emergency deviation. Name the decision owner, revision route, revalidation scope, QA retest, and validity of prior evidence.

## Guardrails

Never treat schedule pressure or implementation completion as risk acceptance. Material changes cannot be hidden in annotations, code, defects, or handoff notes.
