# Synthesize Research Findings

## Goal

Create decision-ready findings without overstating what the evidence can support.

## Method

For each candidate finding:

1. Identify the supporting and contradicting evidence IDs.
2. State the observed pattern or sourced fact without adding interpretation.
3. State the interpretation and plausible alternatives.
4. Explain the implication for the approved decision.
5. Assign `high`, `medium`, or `low` confidence based on evidence quality, consistency, coverage, recency, and limitations.

Preserve recurring behaviors, needs, obstacles, motivations, contradictions, negative cases, and meaningful outliers. Mark each research question answered, partially answered, or unanswered.

## Rules

- Do not infer prevalence from qualitative frequency.
- Do not convert a recommendation into a finding.
- Do not erase evidence that contradicts the dominant pattern.
- Do not create an evidence-backed persona or journey map unless the evidence supports it.
- State exactly what missing evidence limits the decision.

## Output

Create Research Findings using `../templates/RESEARCH-FINDINGS.md`, with evidence-linked findings, question coverage, limitations, recommendations, and next research needs.
