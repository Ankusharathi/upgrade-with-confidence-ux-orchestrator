# Prepare Design Validation

## Goal

Ensure the approved design package tells Validate exactly what must be tested, with whom, under what conditions, and against which criteria.

## Method

1. Identify design hypotheses, highest-risk decisions, consequential actions, recovery paths, and unresolved assumptions.
2. Map each to validation questions, representative roles and contexts, tasks, states, environment, method, success evidence, critical errors, and stopping conditions.
3. Include accessibility and specialist verification needs.
4. Record required prototype fidelity, test data, instrumentation, authorization, privacy, consent, recording, incentive, and storage dependencies.
5. Distinguish formative questions from benchmark or conformance claims.

## Rules

- Do not claim the design is usable because the specification is complete.
- Do not fabricate participants, results, thresholds, or success rates.
- Participant-facing work requires separate authorization.
- Validation criteria must follow from approved outcomes and risks.

## Output

Populate validation readiness in `../templates/DESIGN-SPECIFICATION.md` and the Design handoff.
