# Skill: Execute Design Fidelity Review

## Purpose

Compare an identified implementation build with the exact approved design and implementation specification.

## Method

Verify environment and versions; compare hierarchy, layout, spacing, typography, color, components, tokens, assets, content, responsive behavior, platform conventions, interaction feedback, and motion; capture reproducible expected-versus-observed evidence; classify intentional approved variance separately from defects; and record untestable areas.

## Guardrails

Do not perform a real fidelity review against mock screenshots, a nonexistent build, or unknown versions. Visual similarity does not prove interaction, accessibility, data, or backend correctness.
