# Skill: Conduct Validation Sessions

## Purpose

Execute an approved validation protocol consistently and responsibly while preserving an auditable evidence record.

## Preconditions

- approved Validation Plan and scenario version;
- explicit authorization for recruitment, contact, recording, incentives, sensitive-data access, and session execution as applicable;
- passed or conditionally accepted readiness check;
- consent, privacy, accessibility accommodation, incident, and stop procedures.

## Method

1. Confirm participant eligibility and consent without storing unnecessary identifying data.
2. Identify the tested artifact, environment, session, moderator, observer, and protocol versions.
3. Run the agreed introduction, tasks, probes, and closing consistently; record deviations.
4. Capture observation separately from participant words and moderator interpretation.
5. Record assistance, completion, errors, recovery, time only when validly measured, accessibility barriers, and incidents.
6. Stop or escalate when safety, distress, privacy, consent, data integrity, or environment rules require it.
7. Secure, minimize, anonymize, retain, and delete materials according to the approved plan.

## Prohibitions

Never fabricate missing sessions or reconstruct quotations from memory. Never contact or schedule participants without explicit authorization. A dry run, expert walkthrough, or synthetic transcript must be labeled and cannot count as participant evidence.
